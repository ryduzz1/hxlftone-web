A Discord for manga animation editors.

hxlftone brings manga editors together to share animation edits, editing resources, feedback, packs, and After Effects workflows. The community is built for editors who work with manga panels, anime-inspired motion, typography, compositing, and short-form edit projects.

  • Manga edits and panel animation
  • After Effects resources and workflows
  • Editor feedback, packs, and community projects
